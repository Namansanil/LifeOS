# LIFEOS — PRODUCT VISION

## "How am I living, and am I moving forward?"

LifeOS is a unified personal operating system designed around the person. It bridges six fundamental dimensions of life into a cohesive, intentional loop:

1. **MOVE**: Running, Walking, Cycling, Hiking, Gym, Mobility, and physical activities.
2. **SURF**: Sessions, spots, wave quality, energy levels, and session analytics.
3. **LEARN**: Academic subjects, deep study sessions, assignments, deadlines, and exams.
4. **BUILD**: Software projects, coding sprints, milestones, task kanban, and portfolio creation.
5. **LIVE**: Daily habits, hydration, sleep, reading, morning and evening routines, and journaling.
6. **REFLECT**: Daily reviews with Top 3 focus for tomorrow, weekly reviews, monthly reviews, and truthful behavioral insights.

---

## Core Product Positioning
LifeOS is not a Strava clone, WHOOP clone, Hevy clone, Todoist clone, or Notion clone. Instead, it extracts the most effective patterns from each and centers them on the person:
- **From Strava**: Native GPS activity tracking, sport history, performance statistics, and routes.
- **From WHOOP**: Behavioral readiness scoring, recovery habits, and consistency trends without claiming false medical diagnosis.
- **From Hevy**: Rapid workout logging, sets/reps/weight, progressive overload, and true PR detection.
- **From Todoist**: Low-friction Top 3 priorities, task execution, and deadline clarity.
- **From Notion**: Project hierarchy, deep work tracking, and structured reflection.

---

## Visual & Aesthetic Direction
- **Identity**: Classic Athletic Club + Vintage Surf Journal + Modern Editorial Software.
- **Tone**: Premium, calm, athletic, tactile, mature, editorial, and intentional.
- **Palette**: Warm cream background (`#FAF8F5`), athletic forest green (`#1B3B2B`), coastal navy (`#1B2E3D`), streak amber (`#C25E00`), and crisp white/dark surfaces.
- **Typography**: Editorial typography hierarchy with large numerical metrics, uppercase labels, strong section headings, and restrained metadata.

## The Core Daily Loop
```
PLAN (Morning Top 3 & Readiness)
  ↓
LIVE & TRACK (Activities, Habits, Gym, Surf, Study, Code)
  ↓
REFLECT (Evening Review & Journal)
  ↓
LEARN & IMPROVE (Weekly/Monthly Analytics & Personal Insights)
  ↓
PLAN AGAIN
```
